- Per-collection reader/writer coordination.

- Bounded collection-counted OrderedDict LRU.

- Different collections can load concurrently when capacity permits.
- Identity mismatch waits for active old leases before replacement.
- Evict only idle least-recently-used entries. Never evict active entries.
- Capacity saturation waits only to the configured limit and then raises GatewayBusyError.
- max_graphs=0 loads on every acquisition and retains nothing.
- invalidate removes idle entries or marks active entries for removal on final release.
- summary/status expose safe capacity, counters, lease/revision/method/time/estimated-memory metadata only.
